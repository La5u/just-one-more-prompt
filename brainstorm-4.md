## Heartbeat vs sheep
- "like I used to count sheep" rhymes with "asleep" and ties to sleep (chorus "go to sleep").
- "like it's my heartbeat" loses the rhyme. Compromise: "Now I count my tokens like a heartbeat in my sleep"
- Or keep sheep in the lyric and make heartbeat a visual: the token counter drawn as an ECG line
  that flatlines when the usage limit hits.

## Real quotes that fit (verified sources)
- Pascal, Pensees: "All of humanity's problems stem from man's inability to sit quietly in a room alone."
- Beckett, Worstward Ho (1983): "Ever tried. Ever failed. No matter. Try again. Fail again. Fail better."
- Turing, Computing Machinery and Intelligence (1950): "We can only see a short distance ahead,
  but we can see plenty there that needs to be done."
- Weizenbaum, Computer Power and Human Reason (1976), on ELIZA (1966): "extremely short exposures
  to a relatively simple computer program could induce powerful delusional thinking in quite
  normal people." His secretary asked him to leave the room so she could talk to ELIZA alone.
- "Insanity is doing the same thing over and over and expecting different results": usually
  credited to Einstein, but he never said it. Earliest known source: the Narcotics Anonymous
  Basic Text (1981), i.e. it comes from addiction recovery.
- Frost, Stopping by Woods: "And miles to go before I sleep."
- Camus: "One must imagine Sisyphus happy."
