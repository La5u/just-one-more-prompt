## Session 3c: verse options built from the brainstorm key terms
(4 lines each, can replace or add to verses. Recommended: 1, 6, 4)

1) subagents / parallel / workspaces / summarize
Ten subagents running in parallel
Nine workspaces open and I'm stuck in my shell
They summarize my life in a bulleted list
Three things that went well, and the people I missed

2) shorthand (k / lgtm / lmk)
My vocabulary's shrinking down to "k" and "lgtm"
I tell my friends "lmk", then I never message them

3) swearing / apologize
I swore at it at three a.m. and it apologized
It's the only one who's sorry, it's the only one who tries

4) agents.md / plugins / skills / config tweaking
Rewrote my agents.md for the forty-second time
Optimized the workflow, couldn't optimize my mind
Installed another plugin, wrote another skill
Tuned it to be perfect and I'm tuning it still

5) refactor / overengineering
I refactored my life till there was nothing left to fix
The tests are all passing, I don't know who wrote this

6) definition of insanity / usage reset / productivity
Doing the same thing, waiting for something new
They call it the definition of insanity
I've got a usage limit and it resets at two
I call it productivity

7) dig deeper / exhausted all evidence (from my own chat history)
I said "keep going till you've exhausted all the leads"
It said "the strongest lead is still ahead of me"
It never gets exhausted, it never even sleeps
So the only thing exhausted is me

8) thinking...
It says "thinking..." and I'm thinking too
About everything I'd do if I weren't doing this with you
