## Suno style prompt (draft)
dark pop, doompop, minimal synth, sparse 808, breathy detached female vocal, hypnotic, melancholic, cold, 95 bpm

## What stays out of the lyrics and goes on screen instead
- typed prompts with more and more typos: continue / contiue / conitnue / cintinue / dig deepr / okay continue
- the AI's replies: "The next step is...", "The strongest remaining lead is...", "Public evidence is not exhausted", "Would you like me to keep going?"
- shorthand: k, lgtm, lmk, eta, &, ~, !
- tooling: subagents, parallel, refactor, thinking..., agents.md, skills, plugins, summarize
- usage meter and "resets in 4h 12m"
- feeds and news: "AI proves ...", "CVEs up 400%", "someone shipped X in a weekend" (the FOMO)
- censored swears -> "I apologize."
- "definition of insanity" as a single caption at the very end

## Video concept (minimal, monochrome)
Look: off-white paper, black ink, one serif font + one monospace font. The person is a
single black square. No faces, no photos. A clock in the corner keeps drifting forward.

| Song section   | Screen |
|----------------|--------|
| Intro          | Empty page. A square. A single text box. Clock 16:00. |
| Verse 1        | One chat window. Calm, slow typing, prompts spelled correctly. Small outlines of friends, a window, a door in the background. |
| Pre-chorus     | The paper slowly goes grey, clock jumps to 00:41, then 03:12. |
| Chorus         | "continue" stamped again and again, faster on every beat. An alarm rings, everything turns white again: the next day. |
| Verse 2        | 2 windows, then 4 tiled. A friend's message bubble appears and fades unanswered. Background outlines disappear one by one. |
| Chorus 2       | 9 workspaces flashing past. Usage bar fills; "resets in 4h". Typos increase. |
| Bridge         | Almost black. The AI's lines appear one at a time in serif. "yes" typed three times. The square pushes a boulder (a circle) up the bell curve from the first video, which turns out to be endless. |
| Final chorus   | The calendar flips through days, then weeks, then months. Trophies/diplomas drop next to the square and it doesn't turn. |
| Outro          | Zoom out: the square is one of thousands of identical squares in a grid, each in its own little window, alone. Back to 16:00, one text box. Cursor blinks. Cut. |
