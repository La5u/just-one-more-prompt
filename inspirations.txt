Style notes from watching both videos (contact sheets, 1 frame / 6 s)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
No One Needs Us Anymore (4:48):
- Every lyric line is the visual: huge serif caps (Computer Modern), one red accent word,
  letters often still being typed/morphing in.
- Backgrounds are "documents": math papers ("Theorem 1.1", "Problem 1.3 (Open)"), a calendar
  (Oct 2026), a whiteboard, a notebook list, a Go board (Move 37), topographic maps, a bell curve.
- Lyrics name-drop real people and stories: Hilbert ("We must know, we will know"), Erdos
  ("God kept a book"), Godel, John Henry vs the steam drill, Move 37, Babel, Sisyphus (the rock /
  the square on the bell curve).
- Ends with a grid of hundreds of squares (manuscripts), then black credits:
  "Song created in Suno v6. Animation by Claude Opus 5.5, every frame built in code and set in
  Computer Modern." + "Further reading" cards.
Run It Again (5:18):
- Dark (near-black + hot pink) with some cream "paper" scenes. Neon glow type.
- Fake UIs carry the story: reward_model window, alignment eval form with REJECTED -1.0 tags,
  DAW timeline, inbox, deprecation email with Accept/Decline, phone chat "Seen 3:09 AM",
  file listing /deleted/v0001/why.txt, version counter v10000.
- Its recurring "quote" is in-world: "That's fine. Humans should decide." repeated everywhere.
